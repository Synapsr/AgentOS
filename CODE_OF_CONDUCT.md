# Code of conduct

AgentOS is built in the open, by people who give their time to it. This is
how we treat each other here: in issues, pull requests, reviews, and
anywhere else the project is discussed.

## What we ask

- Be kind, and assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not about the person.
- Say what you know and how you know it. "I tried this and saw that" helps
  more than "this is obviously wrong".
- Make room for people who are new, to the project or to programming. Nobody
  should feel foolish for asking.
- Accept a no gracefully, and give one gracefully.

## What is not welcome

- Insults, mockery, and remarks about who someone is: their gender, origin,
  age, body, religion, orientation, disability or level of experience.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including attention that
  was not asked for.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Anything you would not say to a colleague across a table.

## If something is wrong

Write to the maintainers at **contact@synapsr.io**, the address on the
[Synapsr organisation's profile](https://github.com/Synapsr). Say what
happened and where. It stays between you and them.

The maintainers will read it, ask the people involved if they need to, and
decide what is fair: a quiet word, a warning, removing a comment, or asking
someone to stay away from the project, for a while or for good. They will
tell you what they decided.

Maintainers follow these rules too. If your concern is about one of them,
say so, and the others will handle it.
